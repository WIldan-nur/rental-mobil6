## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the precision, operational velocity, and disciplined authority required for modern enterprise fleet operations. Combining institutional software reliability with an unmistakable, high-end automotive editorial perspective, it departs from conventional, generic corporate blues in favor of an unapologetic, high-contrast palette: deep imperial plum juxtaposed with hyper-legible chartreuse accents.

### Design Movement
The visual language operates on a **Technical Precision & Layered Modernism** foundation. It leverages crisp architectural boundaries, calibrated data density, deliberate surface hierarchy, and high-visibility status indicators. Every touchpoint signals operational control, enterprise-grade data integrity, and premium fleet stewardship.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for operations directors, logistics coordinators, mobility asset managers, and corporate rental analysts. The interface evokes:
- **Absolute Composure:** Instantaneous comprehension of large-scale fleet telemetry and booking pipelines.
- **Prestige Utility:** A commanding visual weight that feels like executive instrumentation rather than consumer utility.
- **Frictionless Velocity:** Razor-sharp typography and unmistakable visual affordances that facilitate critical turnaround decisions.

## Colors

The system employs a tightly calibrated light mode optimized for daylight dispatch environments, long-shift fleet monitors, and executive reporting.

### Primary Spectrum (`#450C3F`)
- **Base Plum (`#450C3F`):** Anchors primary brand anchors, high-level headers, primary action buttons, focused borders, and high-importance UI metrics.
- **Deep Plum Tint (`#2E062A`):** Used for sidebar navigation, table headers in high-density views, and high-priority modals.
- **Subtle Plum Wash (`#F2EAF1`):** Applied to active row selections, active sidebar highlights, and contextual tag backgrounds.

### Accent & Secondary Palette (`#B9D175` / `#D9EFBD`)
- **Vibrant Chartreuse (`#B9D175`):** The operational beacon. Reserved for active vehicle status indicators ("Available", "On Route"), key trend highlights, progress bar completions, and primary interaction pings.
- **Pastel Lime (`#D9EFBD`):** Serves as an auxiliary highlight fill, badge background container, filter chip pill fill, and subtle data visualization segment.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy (`#F5FBDA`)
- **System Canvas (`#F5FBDA`):** A warm, high-end cream base with a delicate lime tint that eliminates sterile stark-white eye fatigue during prolonged screen time.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#FFFFFF`):** Base pure-white container cards to ensure clean content contrast against the tinted canvas.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#FAFCF0`):** Secondary nested containers, card headers, table sub-headers, and input field backgrounds.

### Enterprise Functional Semantics
- **Success / In Service:** `#2D6A4F` text on `#D9EFBD` fill (Vehicle Ready / Returned Clean).
- **Warning / Inspection Due:** `#854D0E` text on `#FEF08A` fill (Maintenance Approaching / Turnaround Delay).
- **Critical / Grounded:** `#991B1B` text on `#FEE2E2` fill (Fleet Incident / Recalled / Immediate Service Needed).
- **Neutral / Idle:** `#4A4550` text on `#E8EAE0` fill (Reserved / Pending Inspection).

## Typography

The pairing combines the executive presence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for display, module titles, and KPI values, with the systematic clarity of **Inter** for dense grids, dynamic parameters, and complex data tables. 

### Data Density & Monospace Accents
Vehicle identification codes (VINs), telemetry readouts (odometer, fuel percentage, GPS coordinates), and financial transactions utilize `mono-data` (JetBrains Mono) to preserve strict tabular alignment across data columns.

### Hierarchy & Legibility Rules
- **Display & Headline Ranks:** Use tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver a compressed, authoritative look suitable for executive KPIs and operational summaries.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Capitalize key status badges and sub-headers with positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.03em`) to maximize scanning speed across 50+ row table views.
- **Numbers:** Enable `tnum` (tabular figures) across all font variants in dashboard tables to eliminate horizontal jitter during real-time telemetry refreshes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a **Dense 12-Column Fluid Grid** with structural responsiveness calibrated for command centers, multi-monitor dispatch stations, and tablet-wielding lot inspection personnel.

### Breakpoints & Reflow Mechanics
- **Desktop Wide (≥1440px):** 12-column dynamic arrangement. Sidebar docked at `280px` fixed width; main workspace occupies remaining space with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer padding. Supports up to 4 simultaneous metric tiles and split-screen asset/map views.
- **Desktop Standard (1024px – 1439px):** 12 columns with standard multi-card distribution. Sub-panels condense into side drawers.
- **Tablet / In-Yard View (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar collapses into an icon rail (`72px`). Tables switch to horizontal scroll with pinned sticky columns (License Plate / Status).
- **Mobile Handheld (<768px):** 4-column layout. Navigation shifts to a persistent bottom application bar. Data tables gracefully break into vertical asset cards.

### Spacing Rhythm
Built strictly on an **8px rhythm** (with 4px increments for micro elements like badge tags and table cells). Dense table rows use `space-xs` (8px) vertical padding, while executive summary cards use `space-lg` (24px) internal clearance to prevent visual fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, opting for **Layered Architectural Tonalism** reinforced by structural ghost borders and plum-tinted ambient glows. This retains an ultra-crisp, high-contrast profile under high ambient glare in car rental dispatch hubs.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Base Flat (Level 0):** Background canvas (`#F5FBDA`). No elevation.
2. **Resting Cards & Modules (Level 1):** Solid white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` low-contrast outline: `rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(31, 26, 36, 0.04)`.
3. **Hover & Interactive Tiers (Level 2):** Elevated interactive vehicle cards and dropdown menus. Outline: `rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.16)`. Ambient shadow: `0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px rgba(31, 26, 36, 0.04)`.
4. **Modals & Overlays (Level 3):** Flyout fleet inspect sheets and dispatch dispatchers. Outline: `rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.24)`. Shadow: `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.18), 0px 4px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
5. **Backdrop Scrim:** Tinted deep plum overlay: `rgba(46, 6, 42, 0.45)` with a `4px` subtle backdrop blur to focus critical lot actions.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a **Soft Industrial Geometry (Level 1)**. This subtle radius balance maintains the structural integrity of dense enterprise tooling while softening the overall look to align with modern automotive body design.

### Dimensional Standards
- **Buttons, Inputs & Interactive Controls:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius. Maintains crisp tactile definition for rapid cursor engagement.
- **Card Containers & Modules:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius (`rounded-lg`). Delineates modular dashboard sections cleanly without wasting grid pixel real estate.
- **Modals, Drawers & Large Flyouts:** `12px` (`0.75rem`) radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pills, Chips & Status Dots:** Fully rounded / pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create an immediate shape distinction between actionable square buttons and passive status tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#450C3F`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `none`. Hover state darkens to `#2E062A` with a micro-lift. Focused with an offset chartreuse ring (`2px solid #B9D175`).
- **Secondary / Action:** Background `#D9EFBD`, text `#2E062A`, border `1px solid #B9D175`. Provides a high-contrast alternative action (e.g., "Assign Fleet Vehicle", "Quick Check-in").
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Background transparent, text `#450C3F`, border `1px solid rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.2)`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#450C3F`, background transparent. Hover: `#F2EAF1`.

### Status Badges & Fleet Chips
Pill-shaped containers (`height: 24px`, padding `0 10px`, font `label-sm`).
- **Active / Available:** Surface `#D9EFBD`, text `#1F4A24`, left-aligned `6px` solid dot in `#5E9E15`.
- **Rented / In Transit:** Surface `#F2EAF1`, text `#450C3F`, dot `#450C3F`.
- **Service / Grounded:** Surface `#FEE2E2`, text `#991B1B`, dot `#DC2626`.
- **Turnaround Clean:** Surface `#E0F2FE`, text `#0369A1`, dot `#0284C7`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Dropdown Inputs:** Surface `#FFFFFF` (or `#FAFCF0`), height `38px` (compact) or `44px` (standard). Border: `1px solid rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.15)`. Text: `#1F1A24`.
- **Focus State:** Border switches to `#450C3F` accompanied by an ambient `2px` ring of `rgba(185, 209, 117, 0.45)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px` width/height. Unchecked: `1.5px solid rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.3)`. Checked: `#450C3F` fill with crisp white internal mark.

### Data Tables & List Views
- **Header Rows:** Surface `#FAFCF0`, text `label-md` `#450C3F`, uppercase tracking, bottom border `2px solid rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.12)`.
- **Rows:** Alternating rows optional; standard uses `#FFFFFF` with bottom border `1px solid rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.06)`. Hover: background `#F9FCED`.
- **Selected Row:** Background `#F2EAF1` with a `3px` left-edge vertical accent bar in `#450C3F`.

### Vehicle Asset Card
Standard module for lot overview:
- Pure white container with `1px` subtle outline.
- Header: Display vehicle make/model in `headline-sm`, right-aligned operational status pill.
- Subtitle: JetBrains Mono VIN tag and current lot zone code.
- Body: Key metadata grid (Fuel/Battery % with dual-color bar, Odometer, Next Service timestamp).
- Footer: Quick-action button group ("Inspect", "Reassign", "Dispatch").

### Specialized Component: Fuel / Range Visualizer
A specialized split horizontal gauge:
- Height `6px`, background `rgba(69, 12, 63, 0.08)`, rounded `9999px`.
- Fill track: `#B9D175` for normal levels, transitioning to `#E11D48` when capacity is below 15%.
